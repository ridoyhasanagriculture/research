Connecting plant science with practical impact.

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Crop Physiology and Ecology at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University, Dinajpur, Bangladesh. My academic and research interests focus on sustainable crop production, plant physiology, natural biostimulants, plant biochemical responses, and environmentally friendly agricultural practices.

Beyond agriculture, I have developed skills in artificial intelligence, digital technology, scientific visualization, photography, cinematography, and data communication. I believe interdisciplinary approaches can accelerate innovation in agriculture and environmental sustainability.

My goal is to contribute to research that connects rigorous plant science with sustainable crop production, supported by thoughtful data analysis and clear scientific communication.
